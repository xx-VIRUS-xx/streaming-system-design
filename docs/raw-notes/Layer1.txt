Adaptive bitrate:
Suppose:
1080p → 8 Mbps
720p  → 5 Mbps
480p  → 2 Mbps
360p  → 800 Kbps
Initially:
Available bandwidth ≈ 15 Mbps
Player choose:
1080p
Then network degrades
Available bandwidth ≈ 3 Mbps
The player observes that its buffer is being consumed faster than it can refill.
It may switch:

720p
  ↓
480p
